The Mission
A Fellowship-flavoured one-shot at 5th level. Familiar D&D mechanics, Middle-earth trappings — if you have played 5e you already know how this works, and if you have not, the app does the sums for you.

Something walked the Old Road three nights ago. No one who saw it will say what it was — only that the trees bent away from it, and the moon didn’t rise until it had passed.

An old man named Rowan finds you on the road, breathless and limping. He has tended a small wayside shrine on the old pilgrim road for forty years — and three nights ago, in the terror left behind by whatever passed through, a warband of Isengard’s orcs overran it.

They are holding the shrine’s one holy relic: the Old Watcher’s Blade, a sword laid there centuries ago, said to never rust and never dull. Rowan fears what they mean to do with it.

“Please. It’s not just steel. It’s the only thing that’s kept this stretch of road safe in my lifetime.”
Pick your character. Rowan is not a patient man.
