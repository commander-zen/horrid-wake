Old Road SoundboardShadow on the Old Road · tap a pad to play, tap a lit loop to fade it

Keys: each pad's key is in its corner. Space cuts everything; Esc fades everything out over 3 seconds.

How sounds behave: a loop on the Music or Ambience channel crossfades out whatever was looping on that channel. One-shots layer on top. A Combo fires several pads at once, which is how BAM drops the crash and the Balrog theme together.

Combat strip: the pads pinned to the bottom each play a random clip from their pool, slightly varied every tap, so rapid hits layer without sounding copy-pasted.

Mixer: the sliders are saved per device, so the phone keeps its own levels.

Before the session: open the board on your phone and tap Arm board. It downloads and decodes every sound and keeps a copy on the phone, so a weak Airbnb signal won't stall the Balrog. Pads marked Stream are long music or ambience tracks: they play straight from the site instead, so they need signal at the table. On an iPhone, flip the silent switch off, or the board stays quiet.
